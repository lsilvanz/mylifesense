Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

MyLifeSense is operated by aiguardu limited (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use MyLifeSense at mylifesense.app, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions? Email admin@aiguardu.app.

MyLifeSense helps you notice patterns in your own data. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. See our Terms & Conditions.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details. If you create an account, your email address and/or your Google account identifier (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase). You can also use the app as a guest without an account.
  • Your tracking data. The Senses, factors and entries you create — the values you log, their dates and times, and any free-text “context” or “About me” notes and text documents you choose to add.
  • Support messages. Anything you send us by email.

Information collected automatically

  • Local storage. If you use the app as a guest, or for preferences (such as your chosen plan cache or focused Sense), we store data in your browser’s local storage on your device.
  • Basic technical data. Standard request information needed to serve the site (handled by our host, Cloudflare). We do not use advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

Information from services you connect

  • Devices & apps (optional). If you connect Fitbit, we read the metrics you authorise (such as steps and resting heart rate) to compute insights. This data is used while you view insights and chat and is not written into your stored entries.
  • Payments. Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We receive your subscription status and Stripe customer/subscription identifiers. We never receive or store your full card details — Stripe handles those directly.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide MyLifeSense — store your Senses and entries, compute correlations and insights, and sync your data across devices when you sign in.
  • To generate AI narratives, chat answers, factor suggestions and to interpret voice or typed logs (see “AI processing” below).
  • To process and manage your subscription and any promo codes you redeem.
  • To send reminders and notifications, if you enable them.
  • To respond to your requests and keep the service secure.

3. AI processing

Some features use a third-party AI provider (Anthropic, “Claude”) to process text on our behalf:

  • Insights, narratives and chat. We send only computed statistical summaries and any context you provided — never your raw entry history.
  • Voice / text log interpretation and factor suggestions. We send the transcript or text you provide, plus the relevant factor labels, so the AI can structure it or suggest what to track.

Anthropic processes this input to return a result and under our API terms does not use it to train its models. You can use the core logging and deterministic insights without AI features.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

5. Where your data is stored

Your account and tracking data are stored by Supabase with row-level security so that each user can access only their own records. Our providers may store and process data on servers located outside New Zealand (including in the United States and the European Union). By using MyLifeSense you consent to these transfers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists or as needed to provide the service. When you delete a Sense or entry, it is removed (including related records). If you want your account and all associated data deleted, email admin@aiguardu.app. Guest data lives only in your browser — clearing your browser storage removes it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to withdraw consent. You can edit or delete your Senses and entries in the app at any time, and export from within Insights. To make any other request, contact admin@aiguardu.app. If you are in New Zealand, you may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

8. Security

We use reputable providers and access controls (including per-user row-level security and encrypted connections) to protect your data. No online service can be perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

9. Children

MyLifeSense is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, take reasonable steps to let you know.
