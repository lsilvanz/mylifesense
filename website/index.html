Why is my abdominal pain worse some evenings?
Your worst evenings cluster on days without morning exercise — pain averaged 8.4 vs 4.7. Late coffee and higher stress tend to show up on those days too.
What should I try first?
Morning exercise has the strongest link (r −0.75). Even a short walk on days you'd normally skip it is a good test — it's a lever you control. Not medical advice; see a clinician for persistent or severe symptoms.
